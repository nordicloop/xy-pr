FAQs

What does a music PR campaign include? +
An XY PR campaign is designed to position your music across the right channels at the right time. Depending on the program, this typically includes press placements, radio and podcast interviews, playlist pitching, social promotion, and SEO-driven media indexing. Each campaign is structured around your release, your stage as an artist, and the outcome you're aiming to achieve.
Do you work with independent artists? +
Yes. We work with distinguished independent artists, managers, and labels at various stages — from early releases to more established campaigns preparing for larger positioning.
How long do campaigns run? +
Campaigns typically run between 3 to 8 weeks, depending on the program selected — Ignition, Social X, or Ascension.
What platforms do you focus on? +
We operate across a mix of media outlets, Spotify, TikTok, YouTube, radio, podcasts, and search engines — building a multi-channel presence that works together rather than in isolation.
How do I get started? +
The first step is a strategy call. We review your release, timeline, and current position, then recommend the campaign structure that makes the most sense for you.
What services does XY PR offer? +
We offer structured PR and marketing campaigns that include media outreach, press placements, interviews, playlist strategy, social growth campaigns, and SEO positioning. Each service is not sold in isolation — it's coordinated as part of a campaign designed to move attention toward the music.
Do you work with all types of music genres? +
Yes. We work across multiple genres. What matters most is having a clear direction, a quality product, and a release we can position properly.
How long does a typical marketing campaign last? +
Most campaigns fall within a 3 to 8 week window. That timeline allows for setup, activation, and visible rollout across platforms.
What kind of results can I expect from your services? +
Our role is to position your music in front of the right audiences and platforms. That includes placements, interviews, and increased activity across channels. Results vary based on the artist, the music, and the level of execution. We focus on creating real opportunities for exposure and growth — not inflated or artificial metrics.
Do you offer one-time services or ongoing packages? +
We offer structured campaign programs. Some clients start with a single campaign, while others continue into multiple phases to build sustained momentum over time.
Can you help with music distribution? +
We do not handle direct distribution. Our focus is on promotion and positioning — driving attention and audience toward the music once it's released.
How do you measure the success of a campaign? +
We measure success through a combination of factors, including media placements, interview coverage, audience growth, engagement signals, and overall expansion of the artist's presence across platforms.
What is your pricing structure? +
Pricing varies depending on the campaign tier and scope. We offer structured programs such as Ignition, Social X, Ascension, and custom campaigns based on your needs and goals.
How do you ensure my music reaches the right audience? +
We align media, playlists, and platforms around your genre, audience profile, and release timing. That includes targeted outreach, curated placements, and distribution strategies designed to put your music in front of listeners who are most likely to engage with it.
Who are Centurion Campaigns for? +
Centurion Campaigns are designed for artists operating at a higher level or preparing for a major push. These campaigns involve deeper media penetration, expanded reach, and a more aggressive rollout across platforms. They are typically suited for artists with strong releases, existing traction, or teams looking to position at a higher tier.
What is the Aladdin Program? +
The Aladdin Program is a fully customizable campaign. Rather than fitting into a fixed structure, it's built around your specific goals, release strategy, and level of ambition. In simple terms — you determine how powerful the genie is. From there, we design the campaign to match.
