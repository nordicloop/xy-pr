Who We Are

About XY PR

XY PR is a music publicity and artist development firm built for one purpose — to position distinguished artists where they are seen, heard, and taken seriously.

We don't approach campaigns as transactions...

We build structured rollouts that connect the artist's story to the right audiences across media, platforms, and culture.

From press placements and radio interviews to Spotify growth and TikTok momentum, every campaign is designed to move attention toward the music in a way that compounds over time.

In a landscape where discoverability is often manufactured and short-lived, our focus is different. We prioritize real positioning, real exposure, and audience growth that holds.

What We Do

We design and execute multi-channel campaigns that align media, platforms, and audience behavior around a single goal: driving attention to the artist.
