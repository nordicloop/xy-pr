What We Do

Music Public Relations
& Artist Marketing

XY PR provides strategic music publicity, media outreach, artist branding, interviews, podcast opportunities, and digital music marketing for independent artists and their teams.

01

Strategic Publicity

High-impact media placement and narrative development.

We craft the narrative around an artist's work and position it where culture is shaped. Top music publications, interviews, and editorial features.

Our team develops long-form storytelling strategies that place artists in front of journalists, editors, and media outlets that define conversations in music.

What this includes:

  • Press campaigns
  • Media outreach and editorial placements
  • Artist interviews and features
  • Publication strategy and narrative positioning
02

Digital Discovery (Streaming + Social)

Building momentum across the platforms where fans discover music.

Beyond press, we design digital campaigns that connect artists with new listeners across streaming platforms and social ecosystems.

Each campaign focuses on audience expansion, fan engagement, and sustained discovery.

What this includes:

  • Spotify and streaming platform promotion
  • Playlist strategy and outreach
  • Influencer and creator partnerships
  • Social media campaign amplification
03

Cultural Positioning & Artist Branding

Transforming artists into recognizable cultural brands.

We help artists shape how they are perceived visually, narratively, and culturally.

By aligning their identity, message, and creative direction, we position artists not just as musicians but as influential voices within their genre and community.

What this includes:

  • Artist narrative development
  • Brand identity and positioning
  • Creative campaign direction
  • Cultural storytelling strategy
04

Campaign Strategy & Launch Architecture

Designing structured campaigns around key moments in an artist's career.

Major releases require orchestration. Our team builds integrated campaign plans around singles, albums, tours, and major announcements to maximize media attention and audience engagement.

What this includes:

  • Release strategy planning
  • Campaign timelines and rollouts
  • Cross-platform marketing strategy
  • Launch event and milestone coordination
